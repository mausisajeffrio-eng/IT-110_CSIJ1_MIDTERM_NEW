Explorer record images
Loaded from the media URLs supplied by each GBIF occurrence. Every detail
dialog displays the provided creator, photo licence and original-photo link
when available. Record-level licences and image licences are distinct.
Absent photographs show a text placeholder instead of a substituted species.
