BIOATLAS PH — DESIGN AND SCOPE

Reference lock
Primary direction: a natural-history field guide adapted to a working atlas.
Preserve: warm paper canvas, dark forest-green ink and controls, editorial serif
headings, compact sans-serif data labels, fine rules and generous map area.
The subject (Philippine biodiversity) and learning brief drive this direction.
Refero live searches were unavailable because the subscription was inactive.
Bundled Refero typography and craft references informed type hierarchy, focus
states, spacing, responsive layouts and keyboard behavior instead.

Decision ledger
- Serif display type: editorial field-guide identity; never on form controls.
- System sans-serif: accessible dense record metadata and controls.
- Green: primary action, selected controls and plant markers.
- Ochre: bird markers and small field-note accents, not body text.
- Real GBIF occurrence photography: evidence, captioned with source and licence.
- Map/list split: supports exploring records and checking their provenance.
- Native dialog and semantic forms: keyboard support without another framework.
- No generic dashboard KPI cards: totals describe records and loaded-page scope.

Stack: ordinary HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Leaflet 1.9.4 and
Leaflet.markercluster 1.5.3 are attributed third-party map libraries.
Scope: two pages, GBIF species/location/year filters, clustered page records,
pagination, details, local saved shortlist, responsive and error states.
