    Reading the atlas

    A little context goes a long way.

    GBIF brings together biodiversity data shared by research institutions, museums, and observation platforms. An occurrence may be an observation, a specimen, or another kind of evidence.

    What the numbers mean

    The search total counts matching records. It does not count unique animals, plants, or species. Each page loads up to 60 records, and only that page is drawn on the map. Clusters show the number of loaded records in an area.

    What is missing

    Searches exclude records without coordinates and records flagged by GBIF for geospatial issues. Some records have no photograph, year, or precise locality. Coordinates may still be uncertain or intentionally generalized. Empty results do not prove a species is absent.

    Locations and dates

    Island-area filters use approximate rectangles, always limited to records assigned to the Philippines. Dates are the event dates supplied by the publisher, not the dates the records were added to GBIF.

    Sources and your shortlist

    Record details link to GBIF and display the available dataset, record licence, and separate photo credit. Shortlisted records are stored only in this browser and may become outdated; follow the source to verify them.
